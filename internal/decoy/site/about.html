About the studio

Harbor Lane began in 2016 as a corner of a shared workshop and a single second-hand wheel. These days it's a small room with good light, two kilns, and far too many test tiles pinned to the wall.

We work in small batches because we like to. It keeps the work honest and lets us chase a glaze for a season without committing a warehouse to it.

Visiting

The studio is open by appointment from Thursday to Sunday. Send a note and we'll find a time — kettle's usually on.
